§1

我们要研究什么?设计什么?What do we study? What do we design?

核心命题:AI 会写代码,为什么仍做不好游戏?——因为游戏的质量不住在代码里,而住在玩法里("quality lives in play, not in code");一句话生成跳过了游戏作为「机制 → 动态 → 体验」系统的整条设计因果链。 Core question: AI can write code — why can't it make good games? Because a game's quality "lives in play, not in code"; one-prompt generation skips the entire causal design chain of Mechanics → Dynamics → Aesthetics.

RQ · 研究问题RESEARCH QUESTION

当 AI Agent 参与游戏创作时,什么样的交互结构与领域知识表征,能让人类设计者保持设计主导权,同时系统性地避免「结构空洞」的生成结果? When AI agents participate in game creation, what interaction structures and domain-knowledge representations let human designers retain authorial control while systematically avoiding structurally vacuous output?

三个假说,以及调研给出的裁决Three hypotheses, and the survey's verdicts

H1强支持STRONGLY SUPPORTED

「一句话 / 凭感觉」做游戏存在本质缺陷One-prompt / vibe-based game making is fundamentally flawed

实证给出机制性诊断:编译通过率与功能正确性反相关;单趟生成 10,400 次零编译成功;项目规模一大即现能力悬崖。失败在结构与架构,不在语法。 Empirical work gives a mechanistic diagnosis: compile-pass rate is anti-correlated with functional correctness; 0 of 10,400 single-pass generations compiled; a capability cliff appears at project scale. Failure lives in structure and architecture, not syntax.

H2支持 · 已成趋势SUPPORTED · NOW MAINSTREAM

应从游戏结构与系统入手构建 AI AgentAgents should start from game structure & systems

2025–26 学界已向「结构化中间表示 + 专门化角色 + 验证回路」收敛。问题不再是"有没有价值",而是"如何差异化"。 Research converged in 2025–26 on structured intermediate representations + specialized roles + verification loops. The question shifts from "is it valuable" to "how to differentiate".

H3部分支持 · 需精确化PARTIALLY · NEEDS PRECISION

通用 AI IDE 不是游戏设计的最佳形态General AI IDEs are not the best form for game design

通用 coding agent 在完整游戏任务上确有天花板(<42%),但原型层尚可。精确论点:通用 IDE 缺三样东西——设计意图表征、领域知识 grounding、playtest 回路。 General coding agents hit a ceiling on complete games (<42%), yet suffice for prototypes. The precise claim: general IDEs lack design-intent representation, domain grounding, and a playtest loop.

因此,我们要设计的是——What we therefore design —

一个结构化、引导式的 AI Agent 游戏制作引擎:以「设计蓝图」为全链路单一事实源,以 MDA 框架为人机共同语言,内建自动 playtest 与多维评估;Agent 定位为知识型协作者,而非替代者。它不替你"生成一个游戏",它引导你做出一个设计。 A structured, guided agentic engine for game making: a design blueprint as single source of truth, MDA as the shared human-AI language, built-in automated playtesting and multi-axis evaluation. The agent is a knowledgeable collaborator, not a replacement — it does not "generate a game" for you; it guides you to produce a design.

技术栈对齐课程:Web 优先(p5.js / Phaser 运行时),后续可扩 Unity 轨道Stack aligned with the course: web-first (p5.js / Phaser runtime), Unity track extensible

§2

当前的科研与产品进度The current research & product landscape

学术侧呈两条谱系:2010 年代的 mixed-initiative 共创工具,与 2023 年后的 LLM 结构化 pipeline——后者正在复刻前者的架构思想。2026 年,「评估层」集中爆发,给一句话生成下了定量诊断。产品侧则呈现"原型可用、完整游戏不可得"的清晰分层。 Research shows two lineages: 2010s mixed-initiative co-creation tools, and post-2023 LLM structured pipelines — the latter now replaying the former's architectural ideas. In 2026 an "evaluation layer" erupted, quantifying one-prompt failure. On the product side: prototypes attainable, complete games not.

当前研究与产品的空白与缺口Gaps & openings

一句话生成的失败遵循一条可定位的链条;而三个结构性空白,恰是这条链条上无人把守的关口。 One-prompt failure follows a locatable chain; three structural gaps sit exactly at its unguarded passes.

G1

引导深度缺口The guidance-depth gap

结构化 pipeline 已收敛,但结构几乎总被当作机器的工程产物(blueprint 给机器看),极少被当作人的决策与教学支架(帮人成为更好的设计者)。ChatGE 的模板引导与 DreamGarden 的计划树触及了,但"引导"未被系统化为一等设计目标。 Structured pipelines have converged, yet structure is treated as engineering artifact for machines, rarely as a decision & teaching scaffold for humans. ChatGE's templates and DreamGarden's plan tree touch this, but "guidance" is not systematized as a first-class design goal.

G2

验证与评估缺口The verification gap

业界痛点是"agent 看不见游戏内部";学界 2026 刚建起评估层(Mage 四轴、GameCraft-Bench、PlaytestArena),但评估从未被整合进创作工具本身。"评估即设计功能"是明确空白。 Industry aches over agents that "can't see into the game"; academia built an evaluation layer only in 2026 (Mage, GameCraft-Bench, PlaytestArena), yet evaluation is never integrated into the creation tool itself. "Evaluation as a design feature" is an open slot.

G3

设计主导权缺口The authorial-leverage gap

创意核心是行业的被回避区(仅 5% 用于玩家向内容);CHI '26 综述把争议归为 agency / authorship / craft accountability。mixed-initiative 时代"建议而非替代"的传统,在 LLM 时代中断了——没有人把主导权当作系统设计的中心约束。 The creative core is the industry's avoided zone (only 5% player-facing use); CHI '26 frames the dispute as agency / authorship / craft accountability. The mixed-initiative tradition of "suggest, don't replace" broke in the LLM era — no system centers authorial control as a design constraint.

三个缺口指向同一个位置:设计意图的表征与协商层——介于"一句话"与"代码"之间、目前无人占据的那一层。 All three gaps point to one locus: a representation-and-negotiation layer for design intent — the unoccupied stratum between "one prompt" and "code".

§4

我们的方案:设计论点种子Our proposal: six design theses

六条论点,每条都有文献或实证依据;合在一起构成引擎的最小架构:蓝图(SSOT)× MDA 对话骨架 × playtest 回路 × 评估面板。 Six theses, each grounded in literature or evidence; together they form the engine's minimal architecture: blueprint (SSOT) × MDA dialogue spine × playtest loop × evaluation panel.

D1

蓝图即单一事实源Blueprint as single source of truth

确立一份人可读写、机器可验证的设计蓝图 schema,全链路生成与修改都锚定它——结构不在对话里流失,意图有处可查。 A design blueprint schema that humans can read/write and machines can validate; all generation and edits anchor to it — structure never evaporates into chat history, intent stays inspectable.

UniGen · G-KMS · Generative Ontology

D2

MDA 即共同语言MDA as shared language

对话按「体验目标 → 动态 → 机制」分层推进:先问"想要什么体验",再反推机制选择。给 Agent 装上游戏设计理论,而不是只装引擎 API。 Dialogue proceeds through "aesthetic goal → dynamics → mechanics": ask what experience is wanted, then reason backward to mechanics. Equip the agent with design theory, not just engine APIs.

Hunicke et al. 2004 · AIIDE '25 语言共同建构

D3

Playtest-in-the-loop 做一等功能Playtest-in-the-loop as a first-class feature

自动 playtest agent + 可玩性 rubric 内建于引擎:每次改动都被"真的玩一遍"。已实证的最大单一增益(+37.1pt),也是行业公认的闭环痛点。 An automated playtest agent with playability rubrics built in: every change is actually played. The largest single empirically-demonstrated gain (+37.1pt), and the industry's acknowledged missing loop.

Play2Code · AutoUE · GDC 2026 观察

D4

评估即设计Evaluation as design

把编译/运行/结构/机制四轴做成用户可见的面板:"哪里空洞"一眼可见,评估从评审工具变成设计工具。 Turn the compile/runtime/structure/mechanism axes into a user-facing panel: vacuity becomes visible at a glance — evaluation becomes a design instrument, not just a review tool.

Mage · OpenGame

D5

可协商的脚手架Negotiable scaffolding

结构必须可绕过、可解释、可协商——实证记录过设计师对"方法论"的抗拒(怕损害创造力)。引导是邀请,不是模具;这是接受度的硬约束。 Structure must be bypassable, explainable, negotiable — designers' documented resistance to "methodology" (fear for creativity) makes this a hard acceptance constraint. Guidance as invitation, not mold.

RMDA 2021 · Sentient Sketchbook

D6

人类作者性优先Human authorship first

人做设计决策,Agent 提供知识、选项与验证。这既是态度实证支持的接受度最优定位,也直接回应 authorship 争议——引擎放大设计者,而非取代。 Humans make design decisions; agents supply knowledge, options, verification. This is the acceptance-optimal position per attitude studies, and answers the authorship dispute — the engine amplifies designers, never replaces them.
