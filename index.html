01 · OPENING QUESTION / 开场问题

AI 会写代码，
为什么仍然做不好游戏？

If AI can write code, why does it still struggle to make a good game?

THESIS / 核心命题

当执行能力不再稀缺，真正困难的是定义目标、组织系统、持续判断与验证。

When execution is no longer scarce, the real challenge is defining goals, organizing systems, exercising judgment, and validating outcomes.

05 · AI LIMITS / AI 的边界

AI 也有结构性的能力边界

AI also has structural limitations.

01

没有创作意图

No intrinsic creative intent

能够模拟方向，但不拥有人类的目的与价值。

It can simulate direction, but does not possess human purpose or values.
02

倾向补全未知

Fills in unknowns

缺失信息可能被流畅而错误地填补。

Missing information may be completed fluently—and incorrectly.
03

局部优于整体

Local over global

能优化模块，不一定维持项目一致性。

It may optimize a module without preserving project coherence.
04

不能体验“好玩”

Cannot experience “fun”

可以分析模式，却无法替代真实玩家体验。

It can analyze patterns, but cannot replace lived player experience.
05

信心不等于正确

Confidence is not correctness

表达确定性的程度不可靠地反映真实性。

Expressed certainty is an unreliable proxy for truth.
PRINCIPLE / 原则

AI 擅长生成与执行，但不能自动承担目标、价值和责任。

AI excels at generation and execution, but cannot automatically assume goals, values, or responsibility.

引文：Bender et al. (2021); Ji et al. (2023); Amershi et al. (2019)。用于支持“流畅输出与可靠性并非同一概念”。 Citations: Bender et al. (2021); Ji et al. (2023); Amershi et al. (2019), supporting the distinction between fluent output and reliable behavior.

09 · LEARNING OBJECTIVE / 学习目标

课程的核心教学目标

The central learning objective of the course

不是让 AI 替学生开发，而是让学生组织一个由人和 AI 共同参与的开发过程。
Not to have AI develop on behalf of students, but to enable students to organize a development process in which humans and AI participate together.

CORE THESIS / 核心论点

AI 游戏开发教育的核心，不是提高内容生成速度，而是培养学生定义、组织、判断和验证复杂创作过程的能力。

The purpose of AI-assisted game development education is not to accelerate content generation, but to cultivate students’ capacity to define, organize, evaluate, and validate complex creative processes.
